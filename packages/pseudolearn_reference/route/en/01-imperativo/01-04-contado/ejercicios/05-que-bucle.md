titulo: Which way of repeating?
---

::: enunciado
Place each task in the way of repeating that suits it best.
:::

```categorias
- You know how many passes
- At least one pass, checks at the end
- Checks first and may make no passes
```

```elementos
- Sing happy birthday 4 times. | You know how many passes
- Ask for the password until it is right. | At least one pass, checks at the end
- Add up the coins in a bag that may be empty. | Checks first and may make no passes
- Show the numbers from 1 to 100. | You know how many passes
- Ask for marks until a -1 arrives. | Checks first and may make no passes
```

::: explicacion
With a known amount, `For` goes. The password is asked at least once: `Repeat`. The bag may be empty and the marks may end with the first value: `While`, which checks first.
:::
