titulo: What shape does each question have?
---

::: enunciado
Each question is answered by walking through a collection of kiosk data. Place each one in the way of solving it that fits.
:::

```categorias
- Walk
- Count
- Gather
- Search
- Filter
- Transform
```

```elementos
- Show each mark on the list. | Walk
- How many days did it rain? | Count
- How much was sold in total? | Gather
- Was there any day above 35 degrees? | Search
- The list of the pupils who passed. | Filter
- All the prices with a 10 % rise. | Transform
```

::: explicacion
Counting and gathering give a number; searching gives an answer and stops as soon as it finds it; filtering and transforming give another collection: shorter the first, the same length the second.
:::
