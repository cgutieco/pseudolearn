::: enunciado
Place each age in its cinema region and say whether it is next to the edge.
:::

```categorias
- Pays 5, far from the edge
- Pays 5, next to the edge
- Pays 8, next to the edge
- Pays 8, far from the edge
```

```elementos
- 3 years | Pays 5, far from the edge
- 11 years | Pays 5, next to the edge
- 12 years | Pays 8, next to the edge
- 65 years | Pays 8, far from the edge
```

::: explicacion
The edge is between 11 and 12: they are the two numbers you must test. 3 and 65 stand for their regions; any other from the same region would do just as well.
:::
