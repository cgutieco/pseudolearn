::: enunciado
On a 3 × 3 board kept with two indices, which position is the centre cell?
:::

```opciones
- [x] `[1, 1]` | Indices start at 0: the centre is the second row and the second column.
- [ ] `[2, 2]` | It would be the centre if indices started at 1; in PseudoLearn it is the bottom right corner.
- [ ] `[1, 2]` | It is the cell on the right edge of the middle row.
```

::: explicacion
In each dimension, the indices of a board of 3 go from 0 to 2. The middle one is 1, in rows and in columns.
:::
