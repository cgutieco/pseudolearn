titulo: Cut or wait?
---

::: enunciado
Place each situation: is it better to cut what is missing, or do you have to wait for everyone?
:::

```categorias
- Better to cut
- Have to wait
```

```elementos
- Looking for a book in three libraries, and it already turned up in one. | Better to cut
- Adding up the sales of three tills. | Have to wait
- Asking two companies for a taxi, and one has already arrived. | Better to cut
- Cooking the three dishes of a tray. | Have to wait
```

::: explicacion
You cut when a single answer is enough. You wait when the result needs everyone, like a sum or a complete tray.
:::
