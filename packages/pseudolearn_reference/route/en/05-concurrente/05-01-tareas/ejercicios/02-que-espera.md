titulo: At once or waiting?
---

::: enunciado
Place each job of the kitchen: can it go at once with the other, or does it have to wait for it?
:::

```categorias
- Can go at once
- Has to wait
```

```elementos
- Washing the fruit and squeezing the juices. | Can go at once
- Charging and handing over the ticket. | Has to wait
- Toasting the bread and cutting the fruit. | Can go at once
- Adding up the tray and handing it over. | Has to wait
```

::: explicacion
Two jobs can go at once if neither needs the result of the other. The ticket is handed over after charging, and the tray is handed over after adding it up.
:::
