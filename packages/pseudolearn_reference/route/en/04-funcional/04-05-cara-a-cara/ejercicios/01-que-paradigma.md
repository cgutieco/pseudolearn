::: enunciado
Place each program in the way of thinking about it that suits it best.
:::

```categorias
- With objects
- Functional
- Imperative
```

```elementos
- A balance that goes up and down all day. | With objects
- A game with characters that keep their energy. | With objects
- Turning a list of sales into a report. | Functional
- Statistics of a class's marks. | Functional
- A 10-line program that converts a temperature. | Imperative
```

::: explicacion
What changes over time and has its own state is well thought of with objects. What transforms data into other data, with functions. A short program that does one thing needs nothing more than orders in a row.
:::
