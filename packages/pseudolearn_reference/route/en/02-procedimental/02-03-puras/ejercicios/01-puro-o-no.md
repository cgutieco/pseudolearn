titulo: Pure or with effects?
---

::: enunciado
Place each job according to whether it can be written as a piece without effects or needs to have effects.
:::

```categorias
- Without effects
- With effects
```

```elementos
- Compute the VAT of a price. | Without effects
- Decide whether a year is a leap year. | Without effects
- Show a welcome message. | With effects
- Read the age of whoever uses the program. | With effects
- Change a box received by reference. | With effects
```

::: explicacion
Computing and deciding only need the data they receive and give back a result. Showing, reading and changing a lent box touch something outside the piece.
:::
