::: enunciado
Read the year you were born and show how old you will turn in 2030. The program shows only the number.
:::

```programa rol=referencia
```

```casos
```

::: pistas
1. Fill in the card: what goes in, what comes out and what sum would you do by hand with 2015?
2. You need a box for the year, and to read it. Look at the greeting in [the lesson](ruta:inicio.preguntar).
3. After reading, a single subtraction is enough.
4. `Read nacimiento;` and `Write 2030 - nacimiento;`. Close with the variant.
:::

::: variante
Also read the current year and show how many years are left until 2030. (With 2026: 4 are left.)
:::

::: explicacion
Hidden cases: being born in 2030 gives 0, and distant years check that no fixed number was used. Frequent mistake: subtracting the other way round and getting negatives.
:::
