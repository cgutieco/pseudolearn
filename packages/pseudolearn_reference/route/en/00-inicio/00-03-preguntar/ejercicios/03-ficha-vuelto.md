::: enunciado
«The kiosk sells biscuits at 2 pesos. A customer pays with a note and buys some biscuits. The kiosk opens at 8. How much change do they get?» Place each piece of data in its zone of the card.
:::

```categorias
- In
- Out
- Fixed value
- Does not matter
```

```elementos
- The value of the note | In
- The number of biscuits | In
- The change | Out
- The price of a biscuit | Fixed value
- The opening time | Does not matter
```

::: explicacion
What changes with every purchase and the customer brings goes in. The answer to the question comes out. The price does not change between purchases: it is a fixed value written in the program. The opening time does not affect the change.
:::
