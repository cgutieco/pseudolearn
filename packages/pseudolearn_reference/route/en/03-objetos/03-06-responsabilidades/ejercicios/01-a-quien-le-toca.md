titulo: Whose job is it?
---

::: enunciado
Place each kiosk job in the piece that has the information to do it.
:::

```categorias
- Product
- Inventory
- Till
- Receipt
```

```elementos
- Knowing the price of a product. | Product
- Computing the amount of a quantity. | Product
- Knowing how much is left of each product. | Inventory
- Taking away from the stock if there is enough. | Inventory
- Gathering the day's total. | Till
- Registering a sale. | Till
- Noting a line of the receipt. | Receipt
- Printing the receipt. | Receipt
```

::: explicacion
Each job goes to the piece that has the data it needs: the price is in the product, the stock in the inventory, what was sold in the till and the lines in the receipt.
:::
