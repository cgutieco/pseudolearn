titulo: What is always true of a lift
---

::: enunciado
Place each sentence about a lift according to whether it has to be true always, at every moment, or is not something the lift has to look after.
:::

```categorias
- Always true
- Not something to look after
```

```elementos
- The floor is between 0 and 10. | Always true
- There are at most 8 people inside. | Always true
- The door does not open while it moves. | Always true
- It is grey. | Not something to look after
```

::: explicacion
An invariant is a rule the object has to keep against any request: the floor, the number of people and the door. The colour is a value, but no method can break it.
:::
