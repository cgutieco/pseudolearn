::: enunciado
A virtual pet has a name, energy and hunger; it can eat, play and sleep. Place each thing according to whether it is something the pet knows or something it knows how to do.
:::

```categorias
- What it knows
- What it knows how to do
```

```elementos
- its name | What it knows
- its energy | What it knows
- its hunger | What it knows
- eat | What it knows how to do
- play | What it knows how to do
- sleep | What it knows how to do
```

::: explicacion
What it knows are its attributes: values that change over time. What it knows how to do are its methods: eating lowers the hunger, playing uses energy, sleeping brings it back.
:::
