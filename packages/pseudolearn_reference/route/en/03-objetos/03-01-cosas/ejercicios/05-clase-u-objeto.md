titulo: The cutter and the biscuit

::: enunciado
With a cutter you make many biscuits, and each one is decorated differently. If the cutter and the biscuit were part of a program, which of the two would describe what they are all like?
:::

```opciones
- [x] The cutter. | It describes the shape they all have; each biscuit is an object made with it.
- [ ] The biscuit. | Each biscuit is one of the things created with the cutter, with its own decoration.
- [ ] Both are the same. | Many different biscuits come out of one cutter: they cannot be the same.
```

::: explicacion
The analogy has a limit: a cutter only gives the shape, and a class also says what each object knows how to do. That is why the figure in the lesson makes it clear in its caption.
:::
